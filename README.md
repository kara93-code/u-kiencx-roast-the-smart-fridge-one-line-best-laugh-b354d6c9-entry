One line, at most 160 characters, roasting a smart home gadget the writer shares a house with. Written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt`. The program holds the chosen line, checks it is a single line of 1–160 characters ending with a period, then writes it.

Chosen line (Version B, 108 characters): "My smart speaker heard me say 'I'm fine' once and now plays sad songs every time I open the fridge at 2 a.m." A smart speaker that is always listening mishears "I'm fine" as a cry for help and answers every 2 a.m. fridge visit with sad songs — the twist lands on the last words.

Entry for the [Roast the Smart Fridge: One Line, Best Laugh Wins](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-roast-the-smart-fridge-one-line-best-laugh-b354d6c9) competition on make.fun.
